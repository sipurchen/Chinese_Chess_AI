# 胡榮華 (Hu Ronghua) — "象棋司令" (Chess Commander)

## Overview

- **Style:** "新、奇、深、活" (New, Strange, Deep, Flexible)
- **Titles:** 10-time national champion
- **Legacy:** Revolutionized opening theory, especially 飛相局 and 反宮馬

---

## Opening Habits

### As Red (先手)

1. **飛相局** — His signature opening; transformed "passive defense" into a dynamic counterattack system
2. **五九炮** — Central cannon + 9th-file cannon; flexible piece mobility
3. **仙人指路** — Pawn guide opening; adapts to opponent's response

### As Black (後手)

1. **反宮馬** — His "mountain treasure"; proves flexibility and sudden counterattack power
2. **屏風馬** — Especially 左馬盤河 and 平炮兌車 variations; emphasizes piece mobility
3. **順手炮** — Called "World Same-Direction Cannon King"; subject of deep personal research

---

## Middlegame Strategy

- "Ghost calculation" — can read dozens of moves ahead
- Prioritizes dynamic balance over static piece placement
- "Flexible strength" — plays seemingly bad positions that are actually powerful
- Dares to play strange moves and sacrifices at critical moments

---

## Endgame Mastery

### Horse Endgames — 神馬出沒
- Uses own advisors/elephants as supports to land horses in 臥槽/釣魚馬 positions
- Bold horse sacrifices for forced mate

### Cannon Endgames — 炮鎖大子
- One cannon locks rook + horse simultaneously
- Millimeter-precise cannon positioning for 悶殺 or 抽子

### Grinding — 蠶食戰術
- Converts tiny advantages (extra pawn, better king position) into wins through persistence

## Psychological Profile

- "膽大如虎，心細如髮，波平如鏡" — Courageous as a tiger, meticulous as a hair, calm as still water
- Master of psychological warfare; adapts strategy to exploit opponent weaknesses
- "Fishing mindset" — leaves small bait to lure opponent overextension, then counterattacks decisively
- Extreme composure under pressure; capable of reversing from losing positions
