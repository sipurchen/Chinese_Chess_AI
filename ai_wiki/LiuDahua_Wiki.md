# 柳大華 (Liu Dahua) — "東方電腦" (Eastern Computer)

## Overview

- **Style:** Aggressive, tactical, force calculation under time pressure
- **Notable Achievement:** Ended Hu Ronghua's 10-championship streak in 1980
- **Nickname Origin:** Machine-like calculation accuracy earned him the title "Eastern Computer"

---

## Opening Habits

### As Red (先手)

1. **中炮盤頭馬** — Most aggressive; uses double-mounted horses for central control, sacrifices pawns for initiative
2. **五七炮進三兵** — Balanced attack on both wings
3. **中炮過河車** — High-pressure style, controls river crossing

### As Black (後手)

1. **屏風馬** — Solid foundation for counterattack
2. **反宮馬** — His specialty; flexible with counterattack traps
3. **單提馬** — Used in rapid games to force calculation fights

---

## Middlegame Tactics

- Loves "chaotic battles" (亂戰) where precise calculation wins
- Sacrifices edge pawns/bottom advisors for key attack lines (以子易先)
- Excels at triple combination: rook + cannon + horse cross-fire
- 閃將 (discovered check) specialist

---

## Endgame Skills

- Near-perfect calculation, "computer-like" accuracy
- Senses hidden instant-kill moves from balanced positions
- Excels at grinding with small material advantages (磨功)
